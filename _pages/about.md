I’m an Assistant Professor at the University of Warsaw, working in the field of cognitive neuroscience. My research focuses on neural plasticity and the ways in which experience shapes the functional organization of the human brain. In particular, I study sign language and the neural mechanisms underlying the interface between vision and language, with an interest in how the brain adapts to different sensory and linguistic experiences.
